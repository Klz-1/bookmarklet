## Detailed Description

**Understand Your Browsing Habits**

Ever wonder where your time goes online? Browsing History Tracker gives you clear insights into your browsing patterns without compromising your privacy.

**Key Features:**

🕐 **Time Tracking**
See exactly how much time you spend on each website. Track daily, weekly, or monthly patterns.

📊 **Visual Dashboard**
Beautiful charts and heatmaps show your activity patterns at a glance. See when you're most active and which sites consume your time.

🔒 **100% Private**
All data stays on YOUR device. Nothing is sent to any servers. Your browsing history is nobody's business but yours.

⚙️ **Full Control**
- Pause tracking anytime
- Exclude sensitive sites (banking, health, etc.)
- Set data retention periods
- Export your data (JSON/CSV)
- Delete everything with one click

🌙 **Dark Mode**
Easy on the eyes with automatic dark mode support.

**Why Choose This Extension?**

Unlike other analytics tools, we believe your data should stay with you. This extension:
- Never connects to external servers
- Never shares or sells your data
- Provides complete transparency (open source)
- Gives you full control over your information

**Perfect For:**
- Understanding your productivity patterns
- Identifying time-wasting websites
- Tracking research sessions
- Personal digital wellbeing

**Permissions Explained:**
- tabs/history: To track which pages you visit
- bookmarks: To detect when you save pages
- storage: To save data on your device
- alarms/idle: For accurate time tracking

Start understanding your browsing today!
