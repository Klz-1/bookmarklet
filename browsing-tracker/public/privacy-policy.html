Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 2025

Summary: Browsing History Tracker stores all your data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or share any of your browsing data.

What Data We Collect

The extension collects the following data about your browsing activity:

  • Page URLs: The web addresses you visit
  • Page Titles: The titles of pages you visit
  • Time Spent: How long you spend on each page
  • Bookmark Events: When you bookmark a page
  • Favicons: Website icons for display purposes

How Data is Stored

All data is stored locally on your device using IndexedDB, a browser database. Your data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not transmitted to any servers
  • Is not shared with any third parties
  • Is not used for advertising or analytics

Data You Control

You have complete control over your data:

  • Pause Tracking: Stop data collection at any time
  • Exclude Sites: Prevent specific domains from being tracked
  • Set Retention: Choose how long data is kept (30 days to 1 year)
  • Export Data: Download your data in JSON or CSV format
  • Delete Data: Remove all data with one click in Settings

Incognito Mode

The extension does not track any browsing activity in Chrome's Incognito mode unless you explicitly grant permission (which we don't recommend).

Permissions Explained

The extension requests these permissions:

  • tabs: To see which page you're viewing
  • history: To access page visit metadata
  • bookmarks: To detect when you bookmark pages
  • storage: To save your data and settings locally
  • alarms: To track time spent (runs every minute)
  • idle: To detect when you're not actively browsing

Children's Privacy

This extension is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.

Browsing History Tracker is open source. You can review the source code to verify these privacy claims.
